## 🕹️ About

ROOM– 24/7 Total Surveillance is an adult single-player simulation and interactive-fiction game developed by Sorarevo and published by Mango Party and Coconuts Entertainment. Set in modern Tokyo, it follows Hiiragi Yume, a rookie news announcer who moves into the condominium where the protagonist works as landlord. Players plan infiltrations, place hidden cameras, observe daily routines, and decide whether surveillance leads toward manipulation, domination, or genuine romance. Branching choices, multiple endings, animated scenes, and illustrated story moments shape each route.

Designed for mature players who enjoy voyeuristic simulation, adult visual novels, relationship choices, and branching stories.

## ✨ Features

- 💔 **Branching Routes** — Pursue paths centered on control, blackmail, affection, or confession.
- 🏢 **Tokyo Condominium Setting** — Follow the private life of a rookie announcer settling into a new home.
- 🔀 **Multiple Endings** — Your decisions determine how each relationship ultimately unfolds.
- 📹 **Surveillance Planning** — Create a careful plan for entering the room and placing hidden cameras.
- 🧩 **Interactive Fiction** — Make choices that reveal secrets and redirect the story.
- 🎨 **Animated Adult Scenes** — Experience illustrated and animated scenes with detailed character expressions.
